Axis Channel Network

This page tracks 135 public Telegram channels run by or aligned with Iran’s “Axis of Resistance”: the Houthis, the IRGC and Iranian state media, Hezbollah, Hamas, Palestinian Islamic Jihad and Iraqi militias. It shows where their messages start and how they spread, how they ask for money, and what each group talks about. Tap or hover any bar for details; sources are in the More section.

Houthi channels start 67% of all copied messages (19,193 of 28,497), mostly passed between their own channels. Across groups, pro-Axis news outlets (mainly Al Mayadeen) are the biggest source (34% of 3,271 cross-group copies), and Houthis lead among the armed groups (22%), feeding the IRGC, Hezbollah and the Iraqi militias most1.

More: shared websites, method and sources

Shared websites. 20 websites promoted by the channels were mapped to their servers. Only one link crosses groups: almoqawma.com, posted by both Hamas and Hezbollah channels, shared server addresses with the IRGC-linked pgsa.ir. Everything else stays inside one group.

Method. A message counts as copied when the same text appears in two or more channels; the first channel to post it is the origin. Same-group copies (for example between Houthi channels) are included in the top bar; the flow chart shows only copies between different groups. Flows under 10 messages are left out.
