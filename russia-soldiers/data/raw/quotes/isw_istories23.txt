ISW, Russian Force Generation Update, 5 June 2025 (search extract of the page, read 28 Oct 2026)
Russian opposition outlet Vazhnye Istorii (iStories) estimated on May 30, using data on Russia’s 2024 federal budget expenditures, that the Russian MoD recruited between 374,200 and 407,200 people in 2024. IStories calculated, using Russian federal data on one-time enlistment payments, that 354,400 people signed military contracts in 2023, as opposed to 540,000 claimed by the Russian MoD in late 2023.
