From Uganda to America: The foreigners dying in Russia's war. By Olga Ivshina, BBC News Russian. Jul 20, 2026 (first published 10 Jul 2026). Read 28 Oct 2026 at https://bbcrussian.substack.com/p/foreigners-in-russias-war
At least 3,589 foreign nationals from more than 40 countries have been killed fighting on Russia's side since its full-scale invasion of Ukraine began, according to a BBC investigation.
Kamwesigye is among 1,285 foreign fighters in the Russian army whose deaths have been verified by BBC News Russian. These includes citizens of Cuba, USA, China, Vietnam, Poland, India, Nepal, Serbia and many others.
A further 2,304 North Korean soldiers were identified by BBC News Korean through satellite images and official photographs of a military memorial in Pyongyang. They were killedfighting Ukrainian troops in Russia's Kursk region after Kyiv launched an incursion there in August 2024.
This scheme primarily affected citizens of former Soviet countries, especially those from Central Asia. For example, more than half of the citizens of Uzbekistan and Tajikistan who were killed in the war were individuals who went to the front from Russian prisons.
Despite the geographical reach of the recruitment drive, foreigners remain a small part of Russia's overall losses. Even including the North Koreans, they account for less than 2% of the 233,033 deaths confirmed by the BBC.
Our list doesn't include residents of the Crimea, Donetsk, Lugansk, Kherson and Zaporizhzhia regions of Ukraine fighting on the Russian side who are counted separately by us, so their losses are not reflected in this summary.
