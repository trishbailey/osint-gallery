Meeting with Defence Minister Sergei Shoigu. October 28, 2022. Starting September 21, the military enlistment offices, together with the executive authorities of the constituent entities of the Russian Federation, have been carrying out the partial mobilisation effort, which involved calling up 300,000 people and deploying them to military units. After completing the training, 82,000 reservists were sent to the area of the special military operation, including more than 41,000 who joined units there. The target you set – 300,000 people – has been achieved. (Text of the Kremlin page read via WebFetch on 28 Oct 2022 page, saved 28 Oct 2026; en.kremlin.ru blocks plain curl.)
