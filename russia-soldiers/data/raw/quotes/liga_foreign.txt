In total, since the start of the full-scale war, 45,959 foreigners from 151 countries and unrecognized territories have fought or are currently fighting as contract soldiers in the Russian army. The updated data clearly demonstrate how the scale of Russian recruitment is growing: → 2022 – 94 contracts; → 2023 – 4,067; → 2024 – 8,877; → 2025 – 16,085; → for the first nine months of 2026 – 16,168. The largest number of foreigners have been recruited in Uzbekistan, Tajikistan, and Belarus. At the same time, recruitment is growing at the fastest rate in countries in Africa and Latin America.
