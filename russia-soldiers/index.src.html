Russia's Soldiers

Where Russia's troops for the war in Ukraine come from. Data as of 28 Oct 2026. First draft.

The Russians killed in the war in Ukraine come disproportionately from regions far from Moscow. This page counts the dead who can be named, by home region, and tests whether distance, low pay or ethnic-minority status explains the pattern. The count is a named list kept by Mediazona and BBC News Russian, built from relatives' posts, local media and official statements.[[mz_list]] It is a floor and not a total: the compilers say it "is not exhaustive, as not every death is publicly reported".[[mz_list]]

The named list undercounts. It holds {{named_total}} names, of which {{named_85}} can be tied to one of the 85 regions drawn here. The other {{outside_85}} come from the Donetsk and Luhansk areas, Baikonur, foreigners and unknown origin. The list leaves out losses of the Donetsk and Luhansk forces.[[mz_list]] Because reporting differs from place to place, a region's rate compares confirmed names, and not true losses.

    Remote: supported. {{cap1}}

    Distance is measured in a straight line from the middle of Moscow city to the middle of each region's largest land area. Pearson's r measures a straight-line link and runs from −1 to 1, where 0 is no link. Spearman's rho does the same for rankings and is less sensitive to a few extreme regions. These describe the data and do not show cause.

            Signing bonuses against local wages

            A contract soldier gets a federal payment of 400,000 roubles, reported by Janis Kluge and shown on VoenSchet's regional cards,[[kluge]] plus a regional payment that each region sets. The regional amounts are read from VoenSchet's summary of regional acts.[[vs]] Each bar adds the two and divides by the region's 2025 average monthly wage,[[wage]] so it shows how many months of local pay the bonus equals. Municipal top-ups and special bonuses for drone troops or reserve units are left out. Where a region's act could not be read, it is missing.

              The recruitment pools

              Four groups feed the army: contract soldiers who sign for money, men called up by decree in 2022, prisoners, and foreign recruits. Each bar starts at zero. A thin line marks a range where the sources give only a low and a high figure. These are different kinds of counts and do not add up to one total.

              No credible estimate

              • The total number of contract soldiers who have signed since 2022: the official count for 2025 is 422,704 against about 364,000 federal bonuses, and no source covers every year since 2022.[[kluge]]
              • The total number of foreigners: Ukraine's list of {{foreigners}} named foreigners is a floor and not an estimate of the total.[[unn]]
              • The total number of prisoners: Wagner's {{wagner}} and the Defence Ministry's Storm Z units are counted separately, and the only all-in figure is a claim by Ukraine's intelligence service that could not be verified.[[ki]]

              How the named dead joined

              Among the 85 regions, the Mediazona pages label each name by how the man joined.[[mz_regions]]

              {{captype}} A recruitment agency's internal target list, reported by The Insider, includes "residents of economically depressed regions".[[insider]] The same investigation found recruiters paid up to 1.25 million roubles per signed contract.[[insider]] Wagner recruited at least 48,366 prisoners in 2022 and 2023.[[ng_wagner]] The North Korean figure is for the Kursk campaign only.[[bbc_nk]]

              Method and limits
              • Rates are named dead divided by the Rosstat population estimate of 1 Jan 2024. Both the list and the population move over time, and the list lags recent months.
              • The map covers 85 regions. Names from Donetsk, Luhansk, Baikonur, foreigners and unknown origin have no regional population or wage and are left out of every rate.
              • Wages are 2025 nominal averages and bonuses are October 2026 rates, so months of wages are slightly overstated where pay has risen since.
              • Bonus status: "firm" means the regional card says the amount was confirmed. Other bars are outlined because the programme ends on 31 Oct 2026, the card data is more than 30 days old, or the amount comes from media reports. Sakha's card gives a range of 1.5 to 3 million rubles regionally and the upper end is charted. Karachay-Cherkessia is shown at the standard 2.1 million rubles, and the card gives 3.1 million for reservists.[[vs]]
              • Regions left out of the bonus chart: {{excluded_list}}.
              • Poverty rates by region were not available from Rosstat, so average wages stand in for income. Wages are not adjusted for local prices, so the wage test is only a rough one.
              • Descriptions only. Nothing on this page is a forecast.
