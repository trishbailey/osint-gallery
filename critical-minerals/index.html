Notes and unknowns
  • Refining shares are not in the USGS data release, so the map and tables above show USGS mining and refining only. The 'From mine to end use' section adds refining and component shares from the International Energy Agency (2025 data), the European Commission (2016 to 2020 average) and the World Nuclear Association, with the year and source on each box. Where no reliable public source gives a share (for example antimony, tantalum and tin refining), nothing is shown rather than an estimate.
  • USGS does not publish world germanium production by country; germanium appears only in the end-use section, with the European Commission's processing share.
  • Codelco is investigating over-reported 2024 and 2025 copper output (October 2026). USGS figures for Chile may later be revised.
  • Data is refreshed yearly when USGS publishes a new Mineral Commodity Summaries release.
