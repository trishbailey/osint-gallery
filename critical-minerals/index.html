Critical minerals: who controls supply

For most of these minerals, one country controls a large share of supply: China leads 7 of the stages shown, and the DR Congo produces 73% of mined cobalt.

Shares are calculated from the USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries 2026 data release, as each country's production divided by the sum of all country rows in the same USGS table, including 'Other countries' (USGS gives no world-total row). Uranium uses World Nuclear Association data for 2024. Refining shares appear only where the source gives a refined-production table (copper, and gallium, whose primary output is refined metal).

From mine to end use

Each row shows the top three countries at each stage and the main uses listed by USGS. This is a stage diagram, not a flow chart: the free sources used do not record how much of each country's ore goes to which refiner, so no flows are drawn or estimated.
