    China's Belt and Road projects

    The Belt and Road Initiative is China's programme of loans, building contracts and stakes in ports, railways, roads, mines and power plants abroad, launched in 2013. This page maps projects that actually happened, region by region: what each one cost, who lent or built it, what China gets and how the host country explains it. Each entry carries a confidence level and its sources.
